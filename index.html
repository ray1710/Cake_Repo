How the porch works

It’s an honour system

Thanks for being kind, honest, and for supporting a small business.

  1. 1

    Look at the list

    Check what’s in the fridge today and pick the flavour you’re craving.

  2. 2

    Open the fridge & grab

    Take the cake (or cakes) you want.

  3. 3

    Close the fridge

    Please don’t stand there with the door open. It keeps the cakes cold for the next person.

  4. 4

    Add it up

    Every cake is $10, so tally up how many you took.

  5. 5

    Pay & enjoy

    e-Transfer or cash, before you leave the porch.

Good questions

The ones everyone asks

Is it actual cake?

Yes. Each tub is layers of cake with a filling and Chantilly cream: vanilla, chocolate, red velvet and so on, depending on the flavour.

Are the cakes halal?

The entire fridge is gelatin-free and alcohol-free. For anything more specific, please message Christina.

Are they nut-free?

No. The fridge contains nuts. This week’s line-up includes peanuts, pistachios and hazelnuts, so please don’t rely on it if you have a nut allergy.

Can I reserve cakes for a party?

The porch is first come, first served. Christina isn’t taking custom orders right now. Seasonal pre-orders, like Thanksgiving, are the exception.

When does the fridge get restocked?

Every day. The flavour line-up changes on Fridays. Follow along on Instagram to see what’s in.

Is the kitchen inspected?

Yes. Christina is food handler certified and bakes in a health-inspected kitchen.

How big are the cakes? Any eggless or dairy-free options?

Best to ask Christina directly. Send her a message on Instagram.
